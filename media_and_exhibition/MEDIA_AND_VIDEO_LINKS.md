This folder documents the Mini Project Exhibition conducted for the Dynamic System Modeling course at NMIMS MPSTME, Mumbai.

Real-time social media documentation was recorded during the two-day exhibition event:

1. **Event Announcement (23 April 2026):**  
   Announcement of the 17 student project teams, SF-PBL framework implementation, and external judge panel logistics.

2. **Day 1 Live Evaluation Update (26 April 2026):**

3. **Day 2 Award Ceremony & Conclusion (27 April 2026):**  
   Announcement of project winners (First Prize, Second Prize, Third Prize, Best Presentation) and judge feedback summary.
