### Comment R2.2: Course Outcome (CO) and Programme Outcome (PO) Attainment Evaluation
> *"This study would be more comprehensive and interesting if the authors also evaluated students' CO achievements. It is also interesting to see the distribution of marks for the COs and POs."*

**Response:**  
We deeply appreciate this insightful recommendation. In response, we have added a comprehensive Course Outcome attainment analysis in both the OBE Framework section (Section 2.2) and Results section (Section 4.6, **Table 6: Course Outcome Attainment**):

1. **Dual-Assessment Framework:** We evaluated attainment across all five course outcomes (CO1--CO5) using two independent empirical sources from our dataset:
   - **Direct Attainment:** Pre-to-post conceptual knowledge gains on domain-specific MCQ items (PK1--PK5).
   - **Indirect Attainment:** Percentage of students demonstrating high self-efficacy ($\geq 4$ on a 5-point Likert scale, i.e., "Agree" or "Strongly Agree") across aligned items (SE1--SE9).

2. **Empirical CO Attainment Findings (Table 6):**
   - **CO1 (Kinematic Analysis, Apply L3):** Direct MCQ: 28.3% $\rightarrow$ 39.6% (+11.3pp); Indirect SE: 3.8% $\rightarrow$ 67.9% (+64.2pp).
   - **CO2 (Free Body Diagrams, Analyse L4):** Direct MCQ: 9.4% $\rightarrow$ 30.2% (+20.8pp); Indirect SE: 3.8% $\rightarrow$ 69.8% (+66.0pp).
   - **CO3 (Newton's Equations of Motion, Analyse L4):** Direct MCQ: 81.1% $\rightarrow$ 96.2% (+15.1pp); Indirect SE: 0.0% $\rightarrow$ 75.5% (+75.5pp).
   - **CO4 (Lagrangian Mechanics, Evaluate L5):** Direct MCQ: 9.4% $\rightarrow$ 71.7% (+62.3pp); Indirect SE: 3.8% $\rightarrow$ 73.6% (+69.8pp).
   - **CO5 (Python ODE Solvers, Create L6):** Direct MCQ: 67.9% $\rightarrow$ 90.6% (+22.6pp); Indirect SE: 4.9% $\rightarrow$ 71.3% (+66.4pp).

3. **Discussion of Attainment Patterns:** We note that CO4 (Lagrangian mechanics) demonstrated the largest direct gain (+62.3 percentage points), aligning with its role as the central focus of Phase 2. We also highlight the pedagogical insight that foundational competencies (CO1, CO2) showed modest direct MCQ accuracy despite strong procedural self-efficacy, identifying them as key targets for enhanced scaffolding in future course offerings.
4. **PO Alignment:** In Section 2.2, we clarified the mapping from CO1--CO5 to NBA Programme Outcomes (PO1: Engineering Knowledge, PO2: Problem Analysis, PO5: Modern Tool Usage) and detailed how the six-judge exhibition rubric dimensions provide multi-perspective authentic evidence for these outcomes.

**Location in Manuscript:** Section 2.2 (Paragraph 2), Section 4.6 (Table 6 and accompanying text).
