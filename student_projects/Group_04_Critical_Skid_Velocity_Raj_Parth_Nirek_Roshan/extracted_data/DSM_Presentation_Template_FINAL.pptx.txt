1. System Introduction & Motivation
This project studies vehicle skidding on curved roads using a model car. 
Skidding occurs when friction is not enough to provide required centripetal force. 
Important for: 
Road safety 
Accident prevention 
Designing safe speed limits 
Problem Statement:At what speed does a car start skidding on a curved path?
