System Description
The selected system is a two-wheeled self-balancing robot model as an Inverted Pendulum mounted on a moving base. The objective of the system is to maintain an upright position by dynamically adjusting wheel motion in response to tilt.

Real-World Applications
Personal Transport Systems
Self-balancing robots are used in devices like the Segway PT, where dynamic balancing allows smooth and stable human transportation.
Warehouse & Industrial Robotics
Used in autonomous robots for material handling, where balance control enables efficient navigation in tight spaces and uneven loads

Problem Statement
The objective is to model and simulate a two-wheeled self-balancing robot, which behaves like an Inverted Pendulum. The system is inherently unstable and requires motor torque input to maintain upright balance. The goal is to derive its equations of motion, simulate the dynamics, and analyze stability under disturbances.

9. Conclusions & Reflections
Conclusion
The 2 DOF self-balancing robot was successfully modeled as an Inverted Pendulum using Newton’s laws.
The derived nonlinear equations of motion were implemented in Python and solved using numerical methods.
Simulation results show that the system stabilizes under control, with tilt angle approaching zero.
Energy analysis confirms non-conservative behavior due to external motor torque input, and sensitivity analysis shows physically consistent response to parameter changes.
Final Outcome:The mathematical model and simulation accurately represent the real system dynamics and validate the effectiveness of the control approach.
