System Description
The selected system is a two-wheeled self-balancing robot model as an Inverted Pendulum mounted on a moving base. The objective of the system is to maintain an upright position by dynamically adjusting wheel motion in response to tilt.

Real-World Applications
Personal Transport Systems
Self-balancing robots are used in devices like the Segway PT, where dynamic balancing allows smooth and stable human transportation.
Warehouse & Industrial Robotics
Used in autonomous robots for material handling, where balance control enables efficient navigation in tight spaces and uneven loads.
Assistive Mobility Devices
Applied in smart wheelchairs and mobility aids to maintain stability and improve user safety on inclined or uneven surfaces.
