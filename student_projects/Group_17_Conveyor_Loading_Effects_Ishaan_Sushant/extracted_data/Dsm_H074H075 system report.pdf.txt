1. System Selection and Approval
The selected system is a conveyor belt transporting a block under Coulomb friction, categorized
under Category B. The system was chosen due to its relevance in industrial automation and its
suitability for applying dynamic system modelling concepts such as Newton’s laws, friction
modelling, and simulation validation.
2. Real-World Application
Conveyor belt systems are widely used in manufacturing, mining, and airport baggage handling.
In these systems, loads are often placed on moving belts, leading to slip, friction-driven
acceleration, and synchronization challenges. Understanding this behaviour is essential for
improving efficiency, reducing material loss, and optimizing system performance.

4. Degrees of Freedom (DOF)
The system has 1 degree of freedom, representing translational motion along the belt. No
rotational or vertical motion is considered, simplifying the system to a point mass model.
5. Free Body Diagram (FBD)
The block is subjected to:
● Weight mgmg (downward)
● Normal reaction N=mgN=mg (upward)
● Friction force:
Ff=μmg⋅sign(Vbelt−v)Ff =μmg⋅sign(Vbelt −v)
When the block velocity is less than belt velocity, friction accelerates the block. Once both
velocities match, friction becomes zero.
