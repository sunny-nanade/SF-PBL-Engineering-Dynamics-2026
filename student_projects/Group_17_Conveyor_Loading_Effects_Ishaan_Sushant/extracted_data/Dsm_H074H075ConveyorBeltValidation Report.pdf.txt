Validation Report: Dynamic Modelling and
Simulation of a Conveyor Belt
1. Objective of Validation
The purpose of validation is to verify that the developed dynamic model of the conveyor belt
accurately represents the physical system and produces results consistent with analytical
calculations and physical laws.
2. Validation 1 – Energy Consistency (CO5)
At steady state, KE = 1/2 mv^2 = 1.125 J. Simulation result » 1.12 J. The simulated value closely
matches analytical computation.
3. Validation 2 – Analytical Time to Reach Belt Speed (CO4)
t = Vbelt/(mg) = 1.5/(0.35×9.81) = 0.437 s. Simulation shows » 0.43 s. Error » 1.6%.
4. Validation 3 – Dimensional Consistency Check
dv/dt = mg. Units on both sides are m/s², confirming dimensional correctness.
5. Sensitivity Analysis
Varying m from 0.2 to 0.5 changes acceleration time from 0.76 s to 0.31 s. Higher m leads to faster
lock-in.
6. Model Limitations
Belt elasticity not considered. Motor torque dynamics excluded. No experimental validation.
Longitudinal vibration ignored.
7. Overall Conclusion
The model is validated through analytical comparison, dimensional checks, and sensitivity analysis.
Results show strong agreement.
