1. Introduction
Reusable rockets are advanced aerospace systems designed to perform controlled vertical
landing after flight. These systems use thrust vector control and feedback-based stabilization
to achieve precision landing.
To analyze the motion and stability of the rocket, a mathematical model is required that
relates forces, torques, and resulting motion.
In this work, the equations of motion are derived using the Lagrangian method, which is
widely used in dynamic system modeling and control.

3. Modeling Assumptions
To simplify the modeling process:
1. Rocket is treated as a rigid body
2. Motion is planar (yaw ignored)
3. Small angle approximation used
4. Thrust acts along body axis
5. Drag is proportional to velocity
6. Mass is constant

5. Choice of Dynamic Modeling Method
The Lagrangian formulation is used because:
• It simplifies multi-DOF systems
• Avoids direct calculation of constraint forces
• Provides a systematic energy-based approach
