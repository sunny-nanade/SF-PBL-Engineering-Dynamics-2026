1. System Introduction & Motivation
System: Vertical Takeoff and Landing (VTOL) 
Rocket Application: Reusable launch systems (SpaceX Falcon 9, ISRO RLV)
Objective: To model and control a rocket that performs stable ascent, hover, and lands back at the same position under disturbances.
Problem Statement: Design a dynamic model and control strategy to achieve stable flight and precise landing of a rocket subject to gravity, drag, and external disturbances.

9. Conclusions & Reflections
Key Finding:A PID-controlled thrust vectoring system can achieve stable hover and precise vertical landing under disturbances such as wind and drag.

What we learned that a textbook couldn’t teach:
Correct sign conventions and force directions in FBD and Lagrange formulation required multiple iterations 
Real systems need controller tuning (Kp, Kd) — theory alone is not sufficient 
Coupling between rotation and translation significantly affects stability (CO4 most challenging) 

What we would do differently:
Implement real-time data logging for accurate plots instead of approximations 
Include variable mass model (fuel consumption) 
Add advanced controller (PID + feedforward / LQR) for better stability

Kinematics of rigid bodies (CO1) 
Free Body Diagrams and force analysis (CO2, CO3)
