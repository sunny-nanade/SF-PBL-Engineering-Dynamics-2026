1. Introduction
This report outlines the validation of an underwater Remotely Operated Vehicle
(ROV) simulation created with PyBullet. The goal is to ensure that the physics in
the simulation reflect real underwater dynamics accurately.
The validation process consists of three main components: comparison with
standard textbook equations, testing energy consistency, and conducting a
sensitivity analysis on key physical parameters—like fluid density and drag
coefficient.
Underwater vehicle dynamics are complex due to the interplay of fluid forces and
rigid body motion. This simulation aims to capture those interactions by using
simplified but meaningful models.

4. Sensitivity Analysis
Sensitivity analysis examines how tweaks in parameters impact system
performance.
Fluid Density:
Raising density enhances buoyancy, letting the ROV rise more easily.
Drag Coefficient:
Increased drag slows speed and heightens energy loss.
Buoyancy Multiplier:
A higher multiplier boosts upward force and stabilizes the ROV.
These results show the simulation reacts realistically to parameter changes.
Sensitivity analysis is key to ensuring the model is robust and dependable.
5. Discussion
The simulation successfully captures essential aspects of underwater motion.
Using quadratic drag models and distributed thruster forces provides a realistic
depiction of vehicle behavior.
The restoring moment is vital for maintaining stability, especially during
disturbances.
However, limitations exist, including simplified geometry and a lack of complex
fluid phenomena like turbulence and vortex shedding.
Despite these limitations, the model establishes a solid foundation for future
development and testing.
6. Conclusion
The validation of ROV simulation was performed based on comparing with
analytical expressions, energy verification, and sensitivity analysis.
It has been shown that the simulated system works realistically from a physical
point of view.
Thus, the model can be successfully used in education and experiments
concerning underwater robotics.
