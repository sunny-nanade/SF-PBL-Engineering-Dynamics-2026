System Identification Report
Topic: Dynamic Modelling of a Sensor Integrated
Remotely Operated Underwater Vehicle for
Environment Modelling and Mapping of Seabed
Aerial/Underwater Dynamics (ROV System)
1. System Selection and Approval
The selected system is a 6-DOF Aerial/Underwater Dynamic System (ROV)
under Category B. This system has been approved by the course instructor
for modeling and simulation.
2. Real-World Application
The system represents underwater vehicles such as ROVs and AUVs used in
ocean exploration, underwater inspection, and autonomous navigation. The
dynamics are governed by thrust, drag, buoyancy, and gravity.
3. System Definition
3.1 Inputs
u = [surge, sway, heave, roll, pitch, yaw]
These inputs are converted into forces and torques using a thruster-based
control allocation system.
3.2 Outputs
Position (x, y, z)
Orientation (roll, pitch, yaw)
Linear velocities (u, v, w)
Angular velocities (p, q, r)
Energy (kinetic, potential, total)
Sensor outputs (sonar distances)
3.3 State Variables
X = [x, y, z, φ, θ, ψ, u, v, w, p, q, r]
The first six represent position and orientation, while the remaining six
represent linear and angular velocities.
3.4 Key Parameters
Mass = 73.89 kg
Fluid density ≈ 1000 kg/m^3
Drag coefficient
Buoyancy factor
Forces: buoyancy, drag, angular drag
3. Degrees of Freedom (DOF)
DOF = 6
Translational: Surge, Sway, Heave
Rotational: Roll, Pitch, Yaw
Justification:
The ROV operates in a three-dimensional fluid environment requiring full
spatial motion. All six axes of motion are independently controlled using a
multi-thruster configuration, enabling complete maneuverability in
translation and rotation.
5. Key Assumptions
In-compressible fluid – Water density is constant
Rigid body – No deformation occurs
Quadratic drag – Drag ∝ velocity²
Constant mass and inertia – No variation during motion
Fixed center of buoyancy – Acts at a constant point
Ideal thrusters – Instant response
No external disturbances – No currents or waves
Simplified hydrodynamics – Added mass ignored
Controlled environment – Motion within bounded region
