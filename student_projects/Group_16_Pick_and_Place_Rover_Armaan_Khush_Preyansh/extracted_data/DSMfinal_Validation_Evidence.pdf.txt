1 TEXTBOOK COMPARISON — DIFFERENTIAL DRIVE KINEMATICS & PLANNING
The drive controller implements the standard differential-drive kinematic model (Siciliano et al., Robotics: Modelling, Planning and Control, 2009, Ch.
11). Body linear velocity v = r·(w_R+w_L)/2 and angular velocity w = r·(w_R-w_L)/b, where r is wheel radius and b is track width. The A* planner follows
Hart, Nilsson & Raphael (1968) with an Euclidean heuristic h(n) = nn-goaln, which is admissible (never overestimates) so optimality is guaranteed. The
IIR smoother is a first-order digital low-pass filter y[k] = a·y[k-1]+(1-a)·u[k], matching Oppenheim & Schafer, Discrete-Time Signal Processing, 3rd ed.
