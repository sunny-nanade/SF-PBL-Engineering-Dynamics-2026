Mobile Manipulation Robot · DSMfinal.py · PyBullet Simulation
1 SYSTEM DESCRIPTION 2 ASSUMPTIONS
The system is a ground mobile manipulation robot simulated in PyBullet A1. Flat, rigid ground plane (plane.urdf); no terrain variation.
at 240 Hz. A Clearpath Husky UGV (4-wheel differential drive, 38 kg)
carries a KUKA iiwa 7-DOF arm rigidly mounted at 0.25 m above the
chassis. The robot executes a full pick-and-place mission: plan a
collision-free route with A*, navigate to an automatically chosen dock beside
the sample cube, grasp the block via a fixed constraint, transit to the goal,
and deposit the block on the green pad.
A2. Physics fidelity fixed at Dt = 1/240 s; real-time playback in GUI mode.
Navigation uses an A* grid planner (0.35 m cells, 0.76 m total clearance) A3. Arm base is kinematically locked to rover chassis (JOINT_FIXED).
with greedy path simplification, segment densification, and a pure-pursuit
heading controller (IIR-smoothed, rate-limited). A 24-candidate dock ring
selects the approach point that minimises path length while orienting the
rover toward the goal direction.
A4. Grasp is ideal: JOINT_FIXED constraint at the end-effector, F_max = 500
N.
A5. Sample block mass = 0.16 kg; all obstacle bodies are static (mass = 0).
A6. Arena bounds are [-7.2, 7.2] m in x and y; planner rejects out-of-bounds
cells.
A7. Obstacle geometry (positions, radii) is fully known at planning time.
A8. Mission timeout = 200 s wall-clock time.
