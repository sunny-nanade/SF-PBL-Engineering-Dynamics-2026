MATHEMATICAL DERIVATION 
Method
 Newton

Why this method
Newton’s rotational law was used because the motor system has direct torque interactions on the shaft, making it easiest to model using a Free Body Diagram (FBD). 
Newton’s method is preferred over Lagrange here because the system is a simple rotating body with minimal constraints, so no need for energy-coordinate formulation. It gives a faster and more intuitive derivation for electromechanical motor dynamics.
