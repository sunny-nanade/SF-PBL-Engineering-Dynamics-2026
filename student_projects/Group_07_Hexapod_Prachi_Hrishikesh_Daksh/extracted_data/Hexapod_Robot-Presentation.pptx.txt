1. SYSTEM INTRODUCTION & MOTIVATION
What is the system? (Real-world description)
A hexapod robot is a six-legged walking robot inspired by insects. Each leg has multiple joints (typically 3 DOF), enabling stable and adaptive locomotion over uneven terrain.
Why does it matter? (Engineering application)
Used in search & rescue (disaster zones)
Suitable for rough terrain exploration where wheels fail
High stability (tripod gait) → always 3 legs on ground
What question are we answering? (PBL problem statement)
To develop a dynamic model and simulation of a hexapod robot and analyze its motion, forces, and energy behavior under different gait patterns. 







2. PROBLEM FORMULATION & ASSUMPTIONS
Inputs:
Joint torques: τ₁, τ₂, τ₃ (per leg)
Control signals (gait timing)
Outputs:
Foot position (x, y, z)
Body displacement and orientation
State Variables:
Joint angles: θ₁, θ₂, θ₃
Angular velocities: θ̇₁, θ̇₂, θ̇₃
Parameters:
Link lengths: L₁, L₂, L₃
Mass of links: m
Gravity: g
Inertia: I
DOF:
Each leg = 3 DOF
Total = 6 × 3 = 18 DOF
 (Justified: each joint rotates independently)
Assumptions:
Links are rigid bodies
Motion analyzed in quasi-static gait (slow walking)
Friction sufficient → no slipping
Actuators provide ideal torque (no delay)

9. CONCLUSIONS & REFLECTIONS
Key Finding
The hexapod robot achieves highly stable locomotion using a tripod gait, while the derived dynamic model accurately captures joint interactions, forces, and energy behavior during motion.
What we learned that a textbook couldn't teach
Understanding how multi-DOF systems behave in real simulations showed us that joint coupling and numerical instability are significant challenges, which are not evident in simplified textbook problems.
Most challenging CO: CO4 (Applying Lagrangian dynamics to a complex multi-body system).
What we would do differently
We would begin simulation earlier instead of completing full derivations first. Not testing it on PyBullet in the earlier stage caused more problem as the leg geometry was correct but at the time of simulation the bot could not stand in proper stance.
Follow a modular approach (one leg -> gait -> full robot).
Course units most critical
Kinematics of multi-body systems
Free Body Diagram analysis
Newtonian and Lagrangian dynamics
Energy methods in dynamic systems
Numerical methods for ODE solving
